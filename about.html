Hi, I'm Aisyah. I'm from Malaysia and I work on machine learning, mostly speech: teaching computers to listen to and speak Malay, and to cope with the way people here mix Malay and English in one sentence.

I'm a research engineer at Revolab AI in Kuala Lumpur, where I train speech recognition and text-to-speech models and spend a lot of time figuring out why they go wrong. Outside work I volunteer with Malaysia AI, an open-source community that trains language models for Malaysian languages.

Before this I did data analytics at Tenaga Nasional, the national electricity company, and studied computer science at University of Malaya.

Things I keep coming back to: low-resource languages, code-switching, how big models are trained, and looking inside a model to understand what it's actually doing.

I write here mainly to explain things to myself. If I got something wrong, or you just want to talk about any of this, email me.
