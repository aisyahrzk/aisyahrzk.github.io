Selected work

Aisyah 1.0 Pro: state-of-the-art Malaysian ASR

2025

Speech recognition for code-switched Malay–English, trained with transformers, self-supervised learning and multilingual transfer. Ranked 1st of 14 commercial and open-source models with 5.79% WER and the lowest hallucination rate (0.62% invented words). Accuracy holds on unseen data: 4.89% on the public split vs 6.58% on the held-out split. Powers Revolab's public Speech Platform API.

Malaysian Speech Benchmark & ASR Leaderboard

2025

1,899 human-annotated samples (4.08 hours) across 12 domains, released as public and private splits, plus a public leaderboard where every model output can be inspected.

Failure modes in autoregressive TTS

2025

Traced repeat/skip failures in Orpheus 3B TTS to three attention heads that track position in the text. Ablating them broke 99.6% of clips, versus 0.9% for three random heads.

MaLLaM: pretraining a Malaysian LLM from scratch

2023–2024

With Malaysia AI: multi-node training on 80× A100 80GB GPUs using Ray and DeepSpeed, crawling and filtering a large-scale Malaysian pretraining corpus, full-parameter fine-tuning of Mistral and Llama, and a novel multimodal pretraining method combining CLIP, Whisper and Mistral.

Safe-for-work classifier for Malaysian text

2024

Led an active-learning + LLM labelling pipeline producing ~200k labelled samples in three languages. Preprint on arXiv, presented at PyCon Malaysia.
