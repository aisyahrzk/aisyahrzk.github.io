AR

I am an ML research engineer at Revolab AI, where I train end-to-end speech foundation models (STT and TTS) for Malaysian and Arabic. I built Aisyah 1.0 Pro, Revolab's Malaysian ASR model, which ranks first of 14 systems on the Malaysian ASR leaderboard.

Since 2023 I have volunteered with Malaysia AI, pretraining Malaysian LLMs from scratch on multi-node A100 clusters and researching multimodal pretraining across audio, image and text. I care about evaluation that holds up on held-out data, and about understanding why models fail, not just that they do.

Interests: speech recognition and synthesis, low-resource and code-switched languages, pretraining at scale, benchmark design, model interpretability.
